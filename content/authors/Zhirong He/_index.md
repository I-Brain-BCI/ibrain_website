Zhirong He is a Ph.D. student in the joint program by Westlake University and the Shenzhen Medical Academy of Research and Translation (SMART). She received her bachelor’s degree from Southern Medical University.

Zhirong's research interests lie in brain-computer interface. With a cross-disciplinary background, Zhirong integrates her deep understanding of neural mechanisms with technical expertise in AI to drive innovations in brain-computer interfaces, with a particular focus on leveraging machine learning algorithms and advanced computational frameworks to decode complex neural signals .

Her work aims to bridge the gap between fundamental neuroscience and engineering applications, creating intelligent neurotechnology solutions that enhance our understanding of the human brain.
