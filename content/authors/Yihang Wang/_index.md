Dr. Yihang Wang is a postdoctoral researcher of the i-Brain Research Group, working on next-gen brain-computer interfaces (BCIs). He received his Ph.D. in Material Sciences from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, and his B.S. in Chemistry from Nankai University.

Dr. Wang’s research focuses on developing next-gen flexible neural implant with minimal invasiveness, high channel density, and multimodal integration. He is also interested in the transformative application of BCIs in both neuroscience and clinical study.
