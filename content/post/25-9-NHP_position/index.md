We are recruiting highly motivated domestic and international researchers at multiple levels to contribute and/or provide leadership in nonhuman primate (NHP) electrophysiology studies on rhesus macaque monkeys. Contact us if you are interested in joining efforts to develop transformative brain computer interfaces (BCIs) for both fundamental NHP studies and as models for human translations. See “[Join Us](../join-us/)” or contact Charles Lieber at liebercharlesm@gmail.com for further information or to apply.
