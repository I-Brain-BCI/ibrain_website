## Position Summary
i-BRAIN is recruiting a highly motivated Research Assistant/Technician specializing in optical imaging and quantitative analysis of cleared and stained rodent and non-human primate (NHP) brain tissue containing intact implanted brain-computer interfaces (BCIs). The successful candidate will support large-scale three-dimensional imaging and analysis of brain tissue to characterize cellular organization, implanted BCI structures, and tissue responses to chronic implantation.

## Core Responsibilities
1. Operate and optimize optical imaging workflows for cleared and stained brain tissue.
2. Acquire high-quality three-dimensional images of rodent and NHP brain samples.
3. Develop and implement image processing and quantitative analysis pipelines.
4. Perform three-dimensional reconstruction, registration, segmentation, and visualization of imaging datasets.
5. Quantify spatial relationships between implanted BCIs and surrounding cellular and tissue structures.
6. Collaborate with tissue clearing and staining researchers to improve imaging quality and reproducibility.



## Qualifications & Requirements
1. B.S. or M.S. degree in neuroscience, biomedical engineering, optical engineering, physics, computer science, or a related field.
2. Demonstrated experience in optical imaging of biological tissues, preferably cleared and stained brain tissue.
3. Knowledge of light-sheet fluorescence microscopy and/or other three-dimensional fluorescence imaging techniques.
4. Experience handling and preparing cleared tissue samples for optical imaging.
5. Demonstrated experience in quantitative analysis of large-scale optical imaging datasets.
6. Familiarity with image processing, three-dimensional reconstruction, image registration, and segmentation.
7. Experience with image analysis software or programming tools such as Fiji/ImageJ, Python, MATLAB, or similar platforms.

## Preferred Qualifications
1. Hands-on experience with light-sheet microscopy of whole brains or thick brain slabs.
2. Experience with high-resolution, large-volume three-dimensional fluorescence imaging.
3. Familiarity with brain atlases, image registration, and automated cell detection or segmentation.
4. Experience managing and processing large-scale imaging datasets.
5. Knowledge of quantitative analysis of neuronal and glial distributions or tissue responses to implanted devices.
