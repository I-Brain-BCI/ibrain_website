## Position Summary
i-BRAIN is recruiting highly motivated Postdoctoral Fellows to develop advanced methodologies for high-resolution three-dimensional optical imaging of rodent and non-human primate (NHP) brains containing intact, chronically implanted brain-computer interfaces (BCIs).
Our research aims to establish comprehensive approaches for evaluating the structural and cellular responses of brain tissue to implanted BCIs, integrating tissue clearing,  cell-type specific labeling, large-scale optical imaging, and quantitative three-dimensional image analysis. These studies will complement ongoing brain-wide activity (BWA) electrophysiological recording and modulation research using advanced BCIs developed at i-BRAIN.
The successful candidate will lead methodological development and interdisciplinary research efforts, with opportunities to participate in BWA studies in rodents and NHPs.

## Appointment and Affiliation
The successful candidate will be appointed as a Postdoctoral Fellow at Tsinghua Shenzhen International Graduate School (SIGS), Tsinghua University, and will conduct research at i-BRAIN.

## Core Responsibilities
1. Develop and optimize advanced methodologies for tissue clearing, labeling, and/or volumetric optical imaging of implanted BCI-whole brains/tissue blocks .
2. Establish quantitative approaches to characterize cellular distributions through the three-dimensional whole brain or tissue blocks containing intact implanted BCIs.
3. Integrate experimental and computational methods for the large-scale and high-through put analyses of different brain-implanted BCI samples.
4. Design and conduct collaborative and/or independent research projects to explore BCI factors affecting critical images analysis results..
5. Collaborate with researchers developing and evaluating advanced BCIs for brain-wide activity studies.
6. Mentor junior researchers and technicians, contribute to scientific publications, and support interdisciplinary research initiatives.


## Qualifications & Requirements
1. Ph.D. degree in neuroscience, bioengineering, biomedical engineering, optical physics/engineering, or a related field.
2. Demonstrated research expertise in at least one of the following areas:
o	Advanced optical imaging, including light-sheet microscopy or related volumetric imaging methods.
o	Quantitative three-dimensional image processing, registration, segmentation, and computational analysis. 
3. Strong experimental or computational problem-solving skills and demonstrated ability to conduct independent research.
4. Experience developing, optimizing, or integrating experimental methodologies.
5. Strong scientific communication skills and ability to work collaboratively across disciplines.
