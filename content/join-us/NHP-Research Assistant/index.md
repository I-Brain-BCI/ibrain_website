Bachelor’s degree or above in relevant fields, such as neuroscience and neurology. Candidates should have extensive laboratory experience in NHP neurosurgery with an emphasis on implantation of chronic electrophysiology probes. A strong sense of responsibility, excellent operational skills, and good communication and teamwork abilities, as well as a proficiency in reading and writing in English are desirable. Outstanding candidates may be recommended for graduate training programs based on their preferences.
