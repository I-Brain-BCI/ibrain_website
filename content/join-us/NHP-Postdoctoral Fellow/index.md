 A PhD degree in neuroscience, neurology or related fields from a leading domestic or international university or research institution or near-term completion of PhD degree. Candidate should have laboratory experience in NHP chronic electrophysiology studies, strong analytical skills and a desire to learn and to develop advanced BCIs that are useful to fundamental NHP studies but have as key goal translation to human patients. Ideal candidates should have demonstrated ability to conduct independent research, a strong proficiency in English communication, reading, and writing, as well as effective communication skills, strong self-motivation, and a keen interest in scientific research. Outstanding candidates can be considered for promotion to more independent positions at i-BRAIN based on their preferences.
