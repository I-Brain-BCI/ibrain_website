## Position Summary
i-BRAIN is recruiting a highly motivated Research Assistant/Technician to support brain tissue clearing and immunostaining for three-dimensional characterization of rodent and non-human primate (NHP) brains with intact implanted brain-computer interfaces (BCIs). The successful candidate will contribute to the development and optimization of tissue clearing and labeling protocols for thick brain slabs and intact whole brains, enabling quantitative evaluation of cellular and tissue responses to chronically implanted BCIs.

## Core Responsibilities
1. Perform and optimize tissue clearing and immunostaining protocols.
2. Prepare rodent and NHP brain tissue for subsequent three-dimensional optical imaging.
3. Evaluate staining quality, antibody penetration, tissue transparency, and sample integrity.
4. Maintain standardized protocols and detailed experimental documentation.
5. Collaborate with imaging and data analysis researchers to improve the overall tissue characterization workflow.


## Qualifications & Requirements
1. B.S. or M.S. degree in neuroscience, biology, biomedical engineering, or a related field.
2. Demonstrated hands-on experience in tissue clearing methodologies for rodent and/or NHP brain tissue, including thick tissue samples or intact whole brains.
3. Experience in immunohistochemistry and immunolabeling of specific cell types and subtypes using monoclonal or polyclonal antibodies.
4. Knowledge of tissue fixation, sample preparation, and methods for improving antibody penetration and staining uniformity in thick tissue samples.
5. Familiarity with other labeling methods targeting cells and macromolecular species.
6. Ability to perform routine optical microscopy for evaluating sample quality and staining results.
7. Strong experimental skills, attention to detail, and ability to maintain reproducible laboratory protocols.

## Preferred Qualifications
1. Experience with automated tissue clearing and immunolabeling systems.
2. Experience in clearing and staining large brain slabs or whole brains.
3. Familiarity with neuronal and glial markers, including NeuN, GFAP, Iba1, and related markers.
4. Experience optimizing protocols to preserve tissue integrity and implanted devices during clearing and labeling.
